# 🏒 NHL Draft Analytics

**Course Project | CSE 163**

Analyzed NHL draft and player performance data to investigate how birth month, physical attributes, junior-league statistics, and playing position relate to draft selection and professional success.

### What I Did
- Cleaned and merged three datasets covering NHL draft picks, professional player statistics, and junior-league performance.
- Aggregated player-level statistics and compared draft position with subsequent NHL performance.
- Visualized patterns in birth month, geography, physical attributes, and player position.

### Tools & Methods
- **Python & Pandas:** Data cleaning, merging, and aggregation
- **Seaborn:** Data visualization and exploratory data analysis
- **Statistical analysis:** Examined relationships between player characteristics, draft position, and NHL performance

### Key Findings
- Observed birth-month patterns consistent with a potential relative-age effect.
- Found that physical attributes appeared more related to draft position than to subsequent NHL success.
- Examined how junior-league scoring and playing position related to professional outcomes.
