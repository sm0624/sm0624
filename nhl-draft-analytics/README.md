# 🏒 NHL Draft Analytics: What Predicts Player Selection and Success?

**Course:** CSE 163  
**Authors:** Srinidhi Muralidharan, Rowen Bertelsen, and Lily Yao

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explored patterns in NHL draft selection and professional player success. Using hockey player and performance data, we investigated how birthplace, birth month, physical characteristics, and junior-league performance relate to draft position and NHL outcomes.

## 🎯 Research Questions

- How are NHL draft picks distributed across countries?
- Is there a relationship between birth month and draft selection?
- How well do junior-league offensive statistics relate to later NHL performance?
- How do height and weight relate to draft position and professional success?
- Which player positions are most represented among first-round picks?

## 🛠️ Tools & Methods

- **Python:** Data cleaning, analysis, and data manipulation
- **Pandas:** Combining and processing multiple datasets
- **Seaborn:** Statistical visualizations
- **Data preparation:** Cleaned and merged NHL draft, NHL performance, and junior-league statistics

## 🔎 Key Findings

- **Birth month:** The distribution of draft picks across birth months suggested a potential relative-age effect.
- **Junior performance:** Junior-league scoring statistics showed relationships with later NHL performance, though their usefulness varied by position.
- **Physical characteristics:** Height and weight appeared more related to draft selection than to subsequent NHL success.
- **Player position:** Centers were prominent among first-round selections.
- **Geography:** The draft data revealed the international reach of NHL player recruitment.

## ⚠️ Limitations

Player success depends on many factors beyond the variables examined, including injuries, coaching, and development opportunities. Scouting and selection biases may also influence the observed patterns. The analysis identifies associations rather than proving causation.
